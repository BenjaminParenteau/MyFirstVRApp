# Visual reference — High Stakes concept art

Descriptions of the concept images in `WHOLEDOCCLAUDEGDIT.pdf`. Use these as the target look. All art is first-person VR POV with dark fingerless-gloved hands and a vignette at the edges.

## Global look & HUD language
- **Two moods:** the main floor is warm gold and green — chandeliers, brass, green felt, glowing slot machines. Back-of-house and the vault are cold blue-teal, concrete and steel, dim fluorescent strips.
- **Suspicion meter:** a round dial in the top-left of the view (Low = green, Medium-Low, High = red) with a needle. Alerts read like "SUSPICION LEVEL INCREASED – PIT BOSS ALERTED".
- **Corner readouts:** chip total bottom-right (e.g. $1,500 / $55,000 Pot Won) with an "Objective:" line beneath (e.g. "Build Bankroll & Gain Trust", "Acquire Passcode Fragment", "Secure The Ledger"), plus a "REPUTATION: VIP Guest" tag.
- **Story arc strip:** Arrival (tutorial night) → Build trust (chips & leads) → Suspicion (security rises) → The vault (heist & ending).

## Storyline
- **Blackjack table (suspicion Low):** player seated at green felt, dealer in black vest and tie, another gambler in a dark suit, chip stacks in front, card shoe on the right, chandeliers and slot machines behind. Objective: Build Bankroll & Gain Trust, $1,500.
- **Back-of-house (suspicion High):** narrow grey concrete corridor, a guard walking away, player holding a STAFF keycard to a wall reader with a green light, wire-mesh door window showing lockers.

## Lore
- **Black Ledger diagram:** Vault → Black Ledger → splits into "The proof (exposes the syndicate)" and "The leverage (Guide's true goal)".
- **Holographic schematic table:** blue hologram floorplan over a desk with a banker's lamp, rotary phone and framed old family portrait. Labels: "The Glittering Front-of-House", "The Underground Economy", a red "Path of the Ledger" arrow linking floor to back rooms. Title: SCHEMATICS: The Second Economy.
- **Black Ledger on desk:** open aged ledger with handwritten names and amounts, same lamp/phone/portrait set. Label: EVIDENCE: The Black Ledger.

## Characters
- **Hero progression strip:** Early game (modest chips) → Chip growth (better tailoring) → Status rises (VIP + overlay) → Full access (fast cash-out).
- **Guide earpiece:** close-up of a sleek silver ear-hook earpiece with a small glowing light. HUD text: "COMM LINK: Active – The Guide", a soft cyan waveform, "Secure Channel".
- **Pit Boss reflection:** player holds a gold compact mirror at the table; in it is the Pit Boss's stern face, guards behind. Wristwatch visible, suspicion dial red ("High Suspicions"). Text: SUSPICION LEVEL INCREASED – PIT BOSS ALERTED.
- **Dealer:** front-facing, black vest, white shirt, dark tie, hands flat on green felt, chip tray in front.
- **Pit Boss:** 50s, swept-back grey hair, grey three-piece suit, burgundy tie with clip, name badge, arms crossed, tablet on a stand showing a table camera feed; a guard in a white shirt stands far behind.
- **Winning-streak diagram:** Winning streak → Comps (free drinks, warnings) or Crackdown (guards tighten up).

## Environments
- **Main floor overview:** wide top-down shot of a huge floor packed with green tables, slot banks and crowd. Mini-map circle top-left labelled "The Meridian Resort – Main Floor" (red marker in centre). Inset panels: VIP Lounge & Back-of-House (isometric cutaway with quiet private baccarat room, VIP access required, staff lockers, security camera blind spot) and The Vault – Restricted.
- **Vault:** cylindrical steel vault door open, walls of safe-deposit boxes, a lectern with the glowing open Black Ledger, cold blue light, "Vault Integrity Meter: Stable" gauge. Objective: Secure The Black Ledger, $55,000 (Pot Won), timer "TIME TO EVACUATE: 14:59 Remaining".

## Puzzles
- **VIP lounge:** dark wood, warm lamps, a private table with a passcode note on the felt, a whiskey tumbler in the player's hand, bar in the background. Objective: Acquire Passcode Fragment; Reputation: VIP Guest; $55,000.
- **Staff roster / keycard logic puzzle (holographic panel in corridor):** three columns — Staff Roster Fragments (torn shift notes), Keycard Data Logs (card ID, code, validity times), Door Access Points (Secure Vault Access, Pit Boss Office). Label: "Logic Puzzle: Cross-reference roster with keycards to open doors."
- **Patrol pattern puzzle (holographic panel at table):** Guard A / Guard B waveform timelines (00:00–08:00, 08:00–16:00), a continuous Camera Sweep wave, live camera thumbnails with a red ALERT on "Corridor 4C – Vault Approach", button "Activate Blind Spot Movement".
- **Environmental cover pair:** left — "DISTRACTION ACTIVE – CROWD COVER ENGAGED / DYNAMIC PATHFINDING" (cocktail waitress, crowd, glowing arrow on floor); right — "CORRIDOR COMPROMISED – NAVIGATING BLIND SPOTS" (guard ahead, cleaning cart, glowing purple path line and keycard).

## Collectibles & progression
- **Collectibles display case:** glass museum case with the Black Ledger open on the bottom shelf, gold card case, keycard tags (Laundry Chute Access, Cleaning Cart Master, Pit Boss Office), passcode fragment scraps, a floor plan with a camera blind spot note, guard shift schedule. Counter "Collectibles Catalog: 8/9 Found".
- **Heist Status & Progression board:** three panels — Access & Area Unlocks (laundry chute keycard → back-of-house; Pit Boss office → VIP lounge; passcode fragments → vault access, 3/4), Intel & Stealth Efficiency (camera blind spot identified / unknown), Chip Totals & Social Status (Ordinary Night → Known High Roller → Valued Associate; $5,200).
- **Progression diagram:** Keycards (opens one door), Passcode (vault code piece), Intel notes (guard patterns), Chip totals (raises status) → Progression (areas, puzzles, status).

## User interface (diegetic)
- **Wrist display:** chunky metal watch with a digital face: BANKROLL: $25,000 / ACTIVE WAGER: $1,000, cyan text on dark.
- **Notebook & card case:** open leather notebook with notes and a card slot holding a keycard, blue screen on the left page; held in a dim corridor.
- **NPC dialogue prompt:** smiling dealer/informant at a table with three floating tap-able options: (A) "Heard about the vault upgrade…", (B) "Just passing through, Vance.", (C) "Any interesting hands tonight?".
- **Diegetic UI flow diagram:** Player → Wrist display (chip count) / Notebook & cards (inventory) / Earpiece & prompts (dialogue).

## Movement
- **Teleport arc:** glowing cyan curved arc from the controller to a landing ring on the floor by a blackjack table; HUD "Movement Mode: Teleport".
- **VR comfort settings panel (held-out wrist panel):** Peripheral Vignette (toggle ON), Movement Mode (Teleport / Smooth), Turn Type (Snap / Smooth), Snap Turn Speed slider, Smooth Turn Speed.
- **Movement diagram:** Movement → Teleportation (open floor blink) + Smooth movement (precise stealth moves) → Comfort settings (vignette, turn speed, swap).

## Sound design
- **Environment strip:** Main floor (lounge score, chatter) → Corridors (tense, thinning score) → Vault (near-silent, low hum).
- **Split image:** floor — clinking chips, murmured conversation, spinning roulette wheel, suspicion meter green; vault side — low electrical hum, distant guard radio chatter, glowing keypad, suspicion rising red. Labels "Soundscape: The Floor (BPM: Low Mood)" and "The Vault (Silence & Hum)".
- **Vertical image:** smooth lounge jazz with music notes and a Pit Boss attention-rising warning triangle. Label "Soundscape: Lounge Jazz (Tempo: Smooth)".
