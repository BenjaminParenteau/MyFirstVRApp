# Vault treasury dressing

Local Ashton feature for the existing `MVP_Vault` room. No shared scene, bar, portal, wallet, puzzle, package or render-pipeline files are edited.

`High Stakes > Vault > Build Treasury Dressing` regenerates `Resources/VaultDressingModule.prefab`, materials and combined meshes in this folder. `VaultDressingModule` installs one instance when `MVP_Vault` loads, at the current room origin `(40, 0, 0)`, and the instance unloads with the area. The room entrance and central aisle stay clear.

Contents: 834 banded cash bundles, 212 beveled bullion bars, 168 safe-deposit drawers, three loaded transport carts, two open cash trunks, a counting station with an open till, a bullion pallet, and a circular reinforced vault-door facade. These are decorative props; they do not award chips, unlock doors or change inventory. The existing rear placeholder and reader remain intact behind/beside the facade.

Geometry is combined by material and empty construction objects are removed. The dressing uses approximately 64k triangles and ten renderers. Collider geometry is limited to substantial furniture. Headset frame time has not been measured.

The project Performance URP configuration disables additional lights. The feature-owned `Treasury Surface` shader therefore uses two fixed local PBR light terms and a generated, mipmapped softbox reflection environment. It supports normal maps and metallic roughness without enabling lights globally, changing the render pipeline, or adding realtime reflection probes. It is authored for this room's current world position.

Materials reuse the project's existing `T_BrushedMetal_Albedo.jpg` and `T_BrushedMetal_Normal.jpg` without modifying those source assets. Paper-edge striping, bevel meshes, drawer/counter details and the reflection cubemap are generated by the builder.

## Generated cash texture

File: `Textures/T_MeridianCash.png`. Created with the built-in imagegen tool, then copied into this project. The note is fictional Meridian Reserve currency.

Prompt:

> Production game texture: one fictional casino reserve banknote, perfectly flat orthographic top-down scan, edge-to-edge rectangular note with width-to-height 2.3:1 centered on a plain matching pale green paper background. Highly detailed engraved green-and-ivory guilloche patterns, central architectural engraving of a grand classical bank vault (not a person), ornate corner denomination 100, readable small title MERIDIAN RESERVE, fine decorative security filigree, realistic subtle cotton-paper fibers, slightly worn corners, faint fold marks. Clearly fictional currency for a 3D casino-heist game, NOT a reproduction of any real country's money. No perspective, no surrounding objects, no cast shadow, no money stacks, no hands, no watermark, no border outside the banknote. Landscape composition. This single diffuse/albedo texture will be mapped to the top face of bundled cash props.
